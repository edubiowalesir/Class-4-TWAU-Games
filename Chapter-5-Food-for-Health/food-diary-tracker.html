👩‍🏫 How to Use

  • Click any Breakfast / Lunch / Dinner box to open the food picker for that meal.
  • Tap the foods you ate — tap again to remove — then tap Done.
  • The summary panel above automatically totals how many Body-Building, Energy-Giving and Protective foods you ate this week.
  • Any food group with 0 foods is highlighted in red — that's a group you need to eat more of!
  • Fill in a whole week, just like the textbook's food diary, and see if your week is balanced.

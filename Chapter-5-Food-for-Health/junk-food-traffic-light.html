👩‍🏫 How to Play

  1. Teams take turns dragging the food card into Green, Yellow or Red.
  2. After every choice, a "Why?" pop-up explains whether it was right and why.
  3. Correct zone → +10 points for the active team.
  4. Turn passes to the other team after each choice, right or wrong.
  5. Sort all 14 foods to finish — see which team made the healthiest choices!

🚦 What the Lights Mean

  • 🟢 Green — Eat Often: Fruits, vegetables, milk — good for everyday health.
  • 🟡 Yellow — Eat Sometimes: Sweets and fried snacks — fine occasionally, not daily.
  • 🔴 Red — Avoid: Packaged, fried and sugary junk food — best avoided.

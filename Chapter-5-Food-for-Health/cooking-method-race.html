👩‍🏫 How to Race

  • Tap Start Race to begin the 60-second countdown.
  • Teams take turns dragging the food card into the bin for its correct cooking method.
  • Correct bin → +10 points for the active team, and the turn passes to the other team.
  • Wrong bin → card bounces back, no points, turn still passes — think fast!
  • Sort all 12 cards before time runs out to win bonus cheers — whoever has more points when the race ends wins!

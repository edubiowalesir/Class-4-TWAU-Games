🎨 Plate Zones

👩‍🏫 How to Play

  1. Drag each food chip from the tray onto the matching zone of the plate.
  2. A balanced thali has protective foods the most, then body-building foods, and only some energy foods — just like in the chapter.
  3. Drop a chip on the wrong zone and it will bounce back — try the correct zone!
  4. Once you've placed several chips, tap Check My Thali to see if your plate is balanced.

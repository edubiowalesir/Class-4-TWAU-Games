👩‍🏫 How to Play

  1. The plate starts full of junk food — fries, burger, soft drink, candy and pizza.
  2. All 8 swap cards are laid out below the plate — drag any one onto the junk food you think it should replace.
  3. Correct swap → the slice turns green, a pop-up explains why it's healthier, and the active team scores +10.
  4. Watch out — 3 of the 8 cards are healthy foods that don't fit any slice. Leave those in the tray and try a different card instead!
  5. Fix all 5 slices to make the plate 100% healthy.

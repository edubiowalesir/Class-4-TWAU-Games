👩‍🏫 How to Play

  • Teams take turns spinning the wheel.
  • When it stops, read the taste and its example foods aloud.
  • The spinning team names one more food with that same taste — if correct, tap +1 for their team.
  • Try to land on all 6 tastes at least once — watch the chips below the wheel light up!
  • Ayurveda calls these six tastes Shad Rasa — a balanced meal includes a bit of all six.

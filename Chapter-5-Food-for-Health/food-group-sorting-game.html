👩‍🏫 How to Play

  • Teams take turns. The active team drags the food card into the bin they think is correct.
  • Correct bin → card lands inside, team scores +10, and the turn passes to the other team.
  • Wrong bin → the card bounces back — try again on your next turn!
  • Sort all 15 cards to finish the game and see the winning team.

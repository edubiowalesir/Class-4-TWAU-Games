👩‍🏫 How to Play

  • Before flipping a card, the active team guesses the grain hidden underneath — or which season it is eaten in.
  • Tap the card to flip it and reveal the grain's name, season, dish and a fun fact.
  • If the team's guess was correct, the teacher taps +1 for that team.
  • Explore all 6 cards to complete the "Super Food Stall" — a small celebration appears when all are found!
  • Tap a flipped card again to turn it back face-down.

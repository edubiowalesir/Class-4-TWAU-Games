🌾🌾🌾

3. HarvestingWhen the crop is golden and ripe, farmers work hard together to cut and gather the grain.
☁️
🐄🛒
4. Threshing & TransportThe grain is separated from the husk, filled into sacks, and carried by bullock cart or truck to the market or mill.
🏭
🌾
🍚
🧑‍🌾
5. At the Mill & MarketWorkers clean and mill the grain, and sellers bring it to the market so it can reach every home.
👩‍🍳
🍲
💨
6. Cooking at HomeAt last, the grain reaches our kitchen, where a family member cooks it with love into a tasty meal.
💛
🧑‍🤝‍🧑
🍛
7. Gratitude at the TableBefore we eat, let's remember the farmer, the animals, and every worker whose effort brought this food to our plate. Say thank you! 🙏

👩‍🏫 How to Use in Class

  • Play each scene aloud as a story — ask students to name who is helping at each stage (farmer, ox, mill worker, seller, mother/family).
  • Pause on Scene 7 and ask: "Who should we thank before we eat our food?" — link to the chapter's gratitude theme.
  • Use the dots below the stage to jump straight to any scene for a quick recap.
